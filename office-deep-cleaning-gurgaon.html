About Office Deep Cleaning in Gurgaon

Here's what a proper deep clean actually involves — and why it matters for your Gurgaon home.

First impressions matter. The condition of your office — sticky desks, stained carpets, grimy pantry and washrooms — says something about your company before a client says a word. More importantly, shared surfaces are the fastest way for germs to move through a team, and a deep clean every quarter measurably cuts sick days.

Our office deep cleaning service is built around your schedule. We work after hours, early mornings or weekends so your team never has to vacate mid-day. We clean and sanitize workstations (desks, monitors, keyboards, chairs), pantries and kitchens, restrooms, glass partitions, reception and conference rooms, and shampoo carpets or scrub tile floors. Our background-checked staff can sign NDAs if your policy requires it. Pricing depends on floor area and scope — we give you an itemized quote before we start.

One crew covers every commercial vertical in Gurgaon: IT and BPO floors, coworking spaces, clinics and labs, retail shops and showrooms, restaurants and cafés, salons, schools, showrooms and warehouses. Clinics get disinfection-grade washroom protocols; food outlets get exhaust degreasing and drain care; IT floors get dry anti-static server-zone methods with your IT team in the loop. Shops and clinics under 1,000 sq ft typically run ₹5,000–₹8,000; larger floors quote per square foot after a free site survey.

We serve every business hub — Cyber City and Cyber Hub, Udyog Vihar Phase 1–5, Golf Course Road and Extension, Sohna Road tech parks, MG Road and DLF Phase 1–5, Sector 32/44/54 corridors, and Manesar/IMT industrial offices. Facility desks get GST invoices with scope lines, staff ID records, chemical MSDS sheets on request and photo-logged walkthrough reports — the documentation audits actually ask for.
