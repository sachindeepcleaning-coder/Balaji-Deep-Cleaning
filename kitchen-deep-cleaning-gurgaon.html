About Kitchen Deep Cleaning in Gurgaon

Here's what a proper deep clean actually involves — and why it matters for your Gurgaon home.

A kitchen is the hardest-working room in any Gurgaon home — and the most neglected during routine cleaning. Daily cooking leaves a stubborn layer of grease on chimney filters, exhaust fans, stove burners, cabinet doors and the tiles behind the hob. That sticky film collects dust and can turn into tough, baked-on grime over just a few months.

Our kitchen deep cleaning service dismantles and degreases the chimney and exhaust fan, scrubs every cabinet inside and out, descales the sink and taps, cleans the stove, hob and microwave, wipes splashback tiles and grout, and finishes with the kitchen floor. We use food-safe, eco-friendly degreasers so your cooking surfaces stay hygienic for your family. A standard kitchen takes 3–4 hours with a 1–2 cleaner team.
