What Affects the Price?

1. Condition of the Property

A home that has had regular maintenance cleaning will cost less to deep clean than one that hasn't been cleaned in months. Heavy grease, mould, or post-construction dust can add to the time and cost.

2. Number of Bathrooms

Each bathroom is cleaned separately. A 2 BHK with 2 bathrooms costs more than a 2 BHK with 1 bathroom.

3. Chimney Type

Built-in modular kitchen chimneys with complex filter stacks take longer to dismantle and clean than basic exhaust fans.

4. Carpet and Sofa Count

Each additional sofa or carpet area adds to the total.

5. Add-Ons

  • Inside refrigerator cleaning: ₹500
  • Balcony deep clean: ₹300–₹500
  • Full window exterior cleaning: ₹800–₹1,500

Why Do Prices Vary So Much Between Services in Gurgaon?

You may see quotes ranging from ₹1,500 to ₹20,000 for "the same" service across Gurgaon. Here's what usually explains the gap:

Cheaper quotes often mean:

  • Fewer cleaners (takes longer, less thorough)
  • No professional machines (only manual scrubbing)
  • Basic products rather than eco-certified ones
  • No quality walkthrough at the end
  • Pay upfront with no recourse if the work is poor

Higher quotes (₹15,000+ for a 2 BHK) from app-based companies usually reflect:

  • Platform commission added on top of cleaner cost
  • Premium brand name pricing (not always better quality)

What you should actually look for:

  • Pay-after-satisfaction policy (we offer this — it protects you)
  • Clear itemized quote before starting
  • Background-checked staff
  • Physical presence in Gurgaon (not a call center elsewhere)

How to Get the Best Deep Cleaning Price in Gurgaon

  1. Book during weekdays — Weekend slots sometimes carry a small premium.
  2. Combine services — Booking kitchen + bathroom together is cheaper per service than booking separately.
  3. Be specific about scope — Tell us exactly how many bathrooms, whether you have a modular kitchen chimney, and the type of sofa fabric. Vague requests lead to quotes with buffers.
  4. Don't book based on price alone — A ₹1,000 saving on a ₹6,000 job is not worth it if the team needs to come back or the work is poor.
