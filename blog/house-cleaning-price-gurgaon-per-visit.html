What Is Not Included in the Per-Visit Price

The per-visit figure covers regular upkeep: dusting, vacuuming, mopping, kitchen and bathroom surface wipe, bins and sanitising of high-touch surfaces. It does not cover chimney dismantling, bathroom descaling, machine floor scrubbing, sofa or carpet shampoo, or cleaning behind and under heavy furniture — those are deep-clean work and are quoted separately. A weekly plan plus one deep clean each quarter is the combination most Gurgaon homes settle into, and the two are never bundled into the per-visit price.

One-Time, Weekly or Bi-Weekly — What the Per-Visit Rate Means

A one-time visit costs more per visit than a scheduled plan because the team is clearing whatever backlog built up before booking. On a weekly or bi-weekly plan the home stays at the same standard between visits, so each visit takes less time and the rate drops. That is why a plan is quoted per visit and not per hour: the price is fixed for your home size, not for however long the visit happens to take.
