Sizing the Job: Crews, Nights and the Sq-Ft Bands

  • Under 1,000 sq ft — 3–4 cleaners, one night, ₹3,999 fixed; walkthrough before your first staff arrive.
  • 1,000–2,500 sq ft — 5 cleaners, 1–2 nights depending on carpet volume; extraction work goes to the Friday night.
  • 2,500–5,000 sq ft — 6–8 cleaners over two nights (Fri + Sat); workstations night one, wet areas and floors night two.
  • 5,000+ sq ft — 10+ cleaners, weekend window, survey-quoted; an AMC handoff audit is included if you run daily housekeeping.

The bands exist because office deeps are logistics problems: crew size and night count are decided by floor plate and carpet volume, then priced — never the reverse. Ask any vendor the two planning questions early: how many cleaners for my size, and which nights. Vague answers to either predict a Monday-morning mess.
