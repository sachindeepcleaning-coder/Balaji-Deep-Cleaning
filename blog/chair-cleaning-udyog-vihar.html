Mesh, Fabric or Leather: Three Different Jobs

  • Mesh-back chairs — low-moisture foam extraction only; soaking mesh warps its tension and the sag is permanent.
  • Fabric seats — hot-water extraction with a fabric-safe rinse; armrests and the seat's front edge carry most of the soil.
  • Leather or leatherette — pH-balanced wipe and conditioner; water extraction cracks leatherette within months.
  • Volume rates — 50+ chairs are quoted per chair at a discount; a 100-chair floor is one night's work for a 4-person crew.

Chairs are also the item office managers most often bundle: add them to a carpet or deep-clean night and the per-chair rate drops because the crew and setup are already on site. Tell us the chair types when you enquire — mesh vs leather decides the chemistry, and quoting without that detail is how damage happens.
