Methods Explained

Dry Cleaning (₹15/sq ft)

  • Dry powder worked into pile → vacuumed out
  • Best for: light soiling, delicate fibres, quick turnaround (usable immediately)
  • Not ideal for: deep stains, heavy allergen load

Shampoo / Hot-Water Extraction (₹18/sq ft)

  • Solution injected under pressure → extracted with dissolved dirt
  • Best for: embedded stains, odour, dust-mite removal
  • Drying: 4–6 hours; removes pet hair, bacteria and deep soil

Fibre Guide — What Can Be Shampooed?

  • Safe (shampoo): nylon, polyester, polypropylene, cotton blends
  • Careful (pH-neutral): wool — colour-safe extraction, tested first
  • Specialist only: Kashmiri, Persian, silk or antique rugs — we assess and may refer to antique rug specialists

What to Expect on the Day

  1. Measure — we confirm area and fibre, test colour fastness
  2. Pre-treat — stains spotted with fibre-safe solution
  3. Shampoo & agitate — worked into pile to lift dirt
  4. Extract — dirty water rinsed and extracted
  5. Dry — airflow speeds drying, we advise ventilation

Drying Science (Why 4–6 Hours Is Normal)

  • Extraction removes ~85% of moisture on the pass; airflow handles the rest
  • Ceiling fan + open windows halve drying time vs sealed AC rooms
  • Winter (Dec–Jan) adds 1–2 hours — book morning slots for same-evening use
  • Walking on damp pile compresses fibres — use the white-sheet trick if unavoidable

Underlay & Edges (Where Smells Actually Live)

  • Pile gets the attention; underlay holds the odour — heavy cases need edge-lift airflow drying, which we do on request
  • Wall-edge lanes (“filtration lines”) grey first near balcony doors — pre-treated as standard, not extra
  • Furniture dents: ice-cube + vacuum lift works on synthetics; wool needs pro grooming to avoid frizz

The fibre decides everything, which is why a proper quote starts with 'what is it made of' — often with the label or a burn-test on a loose thread. Gurgaon homes mostly hold synthetics and cottons, which extract well; wool and viscose need the dry route and cost a bit more because the chemistry and drying control are more demanding.
