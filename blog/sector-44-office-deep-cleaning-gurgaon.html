Why Sector 44 Books Differently

Most vendors sell both the same package; the churn math says otherwise, and our tick-sheet splits them.

Coworking Turnover Cleans (the Sector 44 Special)

Member churn is the dominant cleaning event in Sector 44 shared spaces. A turnover bay gets desk detailing, chair wipe-down, cable-zone vacuum, locker-front wipe and a pantry-proximity check — done in 45–60 minutes per bay in evening slots (7–11 PM) while the floor stays open. Lounge upholstery gets shampoo on the monthly reset, not per turnover. Quote bays individually; a 10-bay turnover evening costs far less than a full-floor night.

Meeting Rooms, Phone Booths & Focus Pods

Sector 44 coworking floors live in their meeting rooms — glass-walled cabins booked back-to-back all day. Corporate floors add projector-lens dusting and cable-tidy checks to the same pass.

Daytime Touch-up Service (While Members Work)

Not everything waits for night. Midday touch-up rounds cover pantry counter wipe-downs, coffee-machine drip trays, washroom consumable refills and bin swaps — quiet hand tools only, no machines, no disruption.
