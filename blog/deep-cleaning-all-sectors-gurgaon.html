Zone-by-Zone — What Deep Cleaning Actually Means There

1. DLF Phase 1–5 — Villas & High-Rise Condos

Large balconies, marble floors and servant quarters. Heavy reliance on AC (dust in vents) and hard water in Phase 4–5. Our team brings marble-safe scrubbers and squeegees shower glass post-descaling. Common booking: 3–4 BHK full-home + sofa shampoo. Same-day from our Golf Course–DLF zone team.

2. Golf Course Road & Extension (Sushant Lok, South City, Nirvana)

Premium society towers (e.g., DLF Magnolias, Ardee). Expectations high — our walkthrough + sanitization step matters. Kitchens are large modular (tall units, pantry pull-outs) — chimney and cabinet degreasing is the bulk of time. Book 9–10 hrs for 3 BHK.

3. Sohna Road Corridor (Vatika, Nirvana Country, South City)

Young families in 2–3 BHK high-rises. Dust from Sohna highway construction settles on balconies and sofa pile. Most bookings: 2 BHK + kitchen + 2 bathrooms (₹4,500–₹5,500). Pre-Diwali fills 2 weeks early.

4. West / Old Gurgaon — Palam Vihar, Sectors 14–22, 45–57, Cyber City, MG Road

Mixed plotted houses and older DDA blocks. Interiors often have Kota stone or vitrified tiles (machine scrub) and older commodes needing descaling. Palam Vihar & Sector 46/47 are our highest-volume west zone. House cleaning weekly (₹499/visit) is popular here alongside quarterly deep cleans.

5. New Gurgaon Sectors 82–95 & Southern Peripheral Road (SPR)

Active construction corridors — cement dust is the defining challenge, penetrating wardrobes and AC filters even in sealed towers. Post-construction dust removal is included in our full-home scope (top-to-bottom wipe, vent clean). Expect an extra hour vs DLF for the same BHK.

6. Manesar, IMT & South-West Fringe

Independent villas and industrial townships. Larger floor plates (5 BHK villas ~₹9,000), open terraces needing terrace drain de-clog + moss treatment (see our balcony/terrace guide). Next-day slots due to distance, no surcharge.
