The Walkthrough Script: Five Minutes That Protect Your Money

  • Start high — fan tops and AC grilles; the most-skipped lines in the industry, easiest to check first.
  • Open everything — wardrobes, cabinets, the chimney housing; insides are where scope cuts hide.
  • Smell tests — kitchen drain and bathroom traps; degreased and descaled homes have no drain odour.
  • Feel the grout — uniform colour and grit-free lines beat any before/after photo.
  • Then pay — the tick sheet signed is the receipt; anything crossed gets redone on the spot, not 'next visit'.

The script exists because quality disputes begin after the crew leaves, when leverage is gone. Five minutes with the checklist in hand converts payment from an act of trust into an act of verification — which is exactly why we structure the terms that way: the walkthrough is the product's final quality gate, not a customer formality.
