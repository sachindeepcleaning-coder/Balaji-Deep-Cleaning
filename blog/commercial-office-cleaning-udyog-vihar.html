Why Udyog Vihar Offices Book After-Hours and Weekends

Udyog Vihar runs on shifts. Cleaning a live floor with staff at the desks is slower, noisier and less thorough, so the work is scheduled after 7 pm or over the weekend. Machine scrubbing, extraction and glass work all need empty floor space. Booking a Friday night for 5,000+ sq ft gives the crew two uninterrupted nights, and everything — including carpet extraction — is dry and back in place before Monday.

What a Site Survey Covers Before a Fixed Quote

  • Floor plate and room count — workstations, cabins, meeting rooms, pantry, washrooms.
  • Floor material — vitrified tile, vinyl, marble or carpet decide the machines and chemicals.
  • Access — service lift, after-hours gate pass, parking for the van and equipment trolley.
  • Water and power points — needed on-floor for machines; agreed in advance with facility staff.
  • Repaint or fit-out plans — if work is scheduled soon, deep cleaning is best booked after it.

What the AMC Does Not Cover (and Why the Quarterly Deep Exists)

  • Carpet and chair extraction — daily vacuuming lifts surface dust; embedded soil in carpet pile and chair fabric needs hot-water extraction, a quarterly job.
  • Washroom descaling — AMC keeps washrooms tidy, but urine scale on urinals and limescale on tapware build up underneath and need acid dwell + scrub every quarter.
  • Glass and facades — internal glass is wiped in the AMC; full glass partitions and hard-water spots on exteriors need a dedicated glass pass.
  • Machine floor work — daily mopping maintains vitrified tile; marble and kota need periodic machine polish that no housekeeping contract includes.
  • High surfaces — ceiling fans, AC vents, light fixtures and curtain rails sit above the daily line and collect a season of dust between deep cleans.
