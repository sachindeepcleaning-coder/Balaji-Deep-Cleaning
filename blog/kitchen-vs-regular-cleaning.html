Why Regular Cleaning Is Not Enough for Gurgaon Kitchens

Indian cooking produces heavy oil vapour and spice residue. In Gurgaon's climate this residue:

  • Sticks to the chimney and exhaust
  • Forms a sticky film on wall tiles and cabinets
  • Attracts dust and becomes a hygiene issue over time
  • Can affect indoor air quality

A daily wipe removes fresh spills. It does not remove the accumulated grease layer that builds over weeks and months.

What Professional Kitchen Deep Cleaning Includes (Balaji Deep Cleaning)

  1. Chimney & Exhaust — Filters are taken out, soaked and cleaned. The entire chimney body and exhaust fan are degreased.
  2. Cooking Area — Hob, burners, under-hob areas, and surrounding tiles are thoroughly cleaned.
  3. Cabinets & Countertops — Exteriors are degreased. Accessible interiors and handles are wiped and sanitized.
  4. Appliances — Fridge exterior, microwave exterior and other appliances are detailed.
  5. Floor & Final Touch — Floor is scrubbed to remove oil film. High-touch points are disinfected.

We use eco-friendly, non-toxic products that are safe for families and pets.

How Often Should You Book Kitchen Deep Cleaning?

  • Heavy daily cooking → every 2–3 months
  • Moderate cooking → every 4–5 months
  • Combined with full home deep clean → every 5–6 months is ideal

What Maids Cannot Do (No Matter How Good)

  • No commercial degreasers — home phenyl cannot dissolve baked oil polymer
  • No dismantling — filters, burners and fan covers stay on, grime stays in
  • No dwell time — degreasers need 15–20 minutes sitting; maids wipe in seconds
  • No extraction/steam — grout lines and traps need machines, not cloth
