Why Sofas Need Professional Cleaning

Your sofa absorbs more than is visible. Gurgaon's fine construction dust settles into fabric daily. Cooking smells penetrate upholstery. Pets, children, spilled drinks and human skin cells create a hidden layer of organic material in the fabric pile that:

  • Feeds dust mites (a leading cause of indoor allergies and asthma)
  • Creates persistent odors that sprays and air fresheners only mask
  • Dulls fabric color and texture over time
  • Can harbor bacteria from food spills

A sofa that looks "clean" at a glance may have years of embedded material that only professional extraction can remove.

Sofa Cleaning Methods Explained

1. Dry Cleaning (₹499/seat)

Uses a dry powder or foam that is worked into the fabric, then vacuumed out with the attached dirt. It is:

  • Best for: Regular maintenance, light surface soiling, delicate fabrics like silk or velvet
  • Not ideal for: Deep stains, heavy odors, significant allergen buildup
  • Drying time: Near zero — the sofa is usable immediately

2. Shampoo / Hot-Water Extraction (₹599/seat)

A cleaning solution is injected into the fabric under pressure, then extracted along with the dissolved dirt and moisture. This is what professional carpet cleaning machines do — adapted for upholstery.

  • Best for: Embedded stains, strong odors, allergen removal, heavily soiled sofas
  • Drying time: 4–6 hours in normal room conditions
  • Suction power removes: Dust mites, pet hair, bacteria, years of embedded soil

3. Steam Cleaning

A variant of the hot-water extraction method using steam at higher temperatures. More effective at killing bacteria and dust mites. Balaji Deep Cleaning uses this for mattresses and heavily contaminated upholstery.

Fabric Guide: What Can Be Shampooed?

Safe for shampoo extraction:

  • Cotton and cotton blends
  • Polyester and synthetic blends
  • Microfibre / microsuede
  • Nylon

Requires special care (low moisture method):

  • Velvet (flattens easily — we use low-pressure, low-moisture extraction)
  • Jacquard (intricate weave — cleaned with care)
  • Linen (prone to shrinkage — checked first)

Dry clean only (no water):

  • Silk upholstery
  • Antique / vintage fabric
  • Any fabric labelled "W" (water-safe) is fine for shampoo; "S" (solvent only) — dry clean only; "X" — vacuum only

Leather sofas

Leather is cleaned with a leather-safe conditioner cleaner — never shampoo or water extraction. We include leather sofa cleaning as a separate service.

What to Do Before the Cleaner Arrives

  1. Remove cushion covers that are machine-washable (they can be washed while the sofa is being cleaned)
  2. Remove decorative throws, pillows and any items on or around the sofa
  3. Ensure good ventilation in the room (open windows or run a fan) — this helps the sofa dry faster
  4. Point out any specific stain areas and the type of stain if you know it (food, wine, ink, pet urine) — treatment varies

How Long Until the Sofa is Dry?

After hot-water extraction: 4–6 hours under normal room conditions. Faster with a ceiling fan or open windows. In Gurgaon's winter months (December–January), add 1–2 hours due to lower humidity absorption. The sofa is usable the same evening in most cases.

Do not sit on a damp sofa — it compresses the fabric while wet and can create new staining from body pressure. Place a clean white bedsheet over it if children or pets must use it before it's fully dry.

Between Professional Cleans: What Keeps Fabric Good

  • Vacuum monthly, crevice tool out — sofa insides and under-cushion crumbs are what actually degrade fabric and feed odours.
  • Rotate and flip cushions — even wear delays the seat-shape and sheen that make sofas look old before they are.
  • Blot, never rub, spills — rubbing pushes soil deeper and fuzzes the pile; blot from the outside of the stain inward.
  • Sun strategy — direct afternoon sun fades fabric unevenly; a sheer or a rotation habit buys years.
  • Annual extraction — one professional wash a year resets what home care maintains; twice for homes with kids or pets.

The ₹499-per-seat wash gives the best return when the fabric arrives vacuumed and freshly maintained — extraction lifts soil, not half a year of crumbs. That is also why the technician tests and pre-sprays: the difference between a refreshed sofa and a damp, tired one is chemistry and dwell time, not scrubbing pressure.
