Stains, Odour, Kids, Pets and Allergies

  • Sweat yellowing — the most common Gurgaon mattress stain; enzyme + extraction usually restores it well
  • Kids and pets — urine and pet odour need enzyme dwell before extraction; masking sprays only pause the smell
  • Allergies and asthma — extraction removes mites and droppings vacuuming leaves behind; pair with pillow and under-bed detail
  • Old vs fresh — fresh spills lift far better than year-old sets; treat fast, photograph honestly, and never bleach a mattress top
  • What we refuse — torn, mould-eaten or structurally collapsed mattresses are flagged, not “cleaned”; replacement advice beats a wasted visit

How Often a Gurgaon Mattress Needs Extraction

  • Adults, no pets — every 12 months with monthly vacuuming between.
  • Kids, pets, allergies — every 6 months; protector washed monthly.
  • After dust events — renovation, move-in, or a Damp-season spell: book once, then resume schedule.
  • Between visits — vacuum monthly, rotate head-to-foot quarterly, protector always on.

Sheets hide the mattress, not its soil. One extraction a year — timed before winter or after the monsoon — keeps the bed you spend a third of your life on genuinely clean, not just covered. Pair it with the sofa visit and both soft furnishings reset inside a single crew window.
