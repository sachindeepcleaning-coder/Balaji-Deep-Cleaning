One Contract or Three? How Gurgaon Businesses Actually Buy

  • Shops and clinics — a weekly deep plus daily upkeep hybrid; predictable, no survey needed.
  • Offices under 2,500 sq ft — quarterly deep plus a light AMC; one vendor, one invoice, one gate pass.
  • Campus and multi-floor offices — separate AMC and deep-clean vendors is common, but one vendor with both teams avoids blame games between contracts.
  • Fit-out handovers — post-construction first, then AMC from day one; bundling both with the AMC vendor gets the handover audit free.

Whichever shape you choose, insist on the same three documents: a written per-sq-ft or per-visit rate, a checklist signed at each visit, and a named supervisor who answers the phone. Vendors who resist any of the three are pricing for churn, not retention.

What a Day-One AMC Handover Includes

  • Site map with zone ownership — each cleaner owns named zones, so nothing falls between two people.
  • Consumables audit — what the client supplies (usually tissue, handwash refills) versus what we bring (chemicals, mops, liners).
  • Attendance protocol — a photo check-in on the group WhatsApp; a no-show is escalated within 30 minutes, not discovered at 6 pm.
  • Monthly review — 15 minutes with your admin on gaps found and fixes; AMC terms are adjusted at review, not mid-month.
  • Deep-clean calendar — the quarterly dates are booked a year ahead so they never get squeezed out.

Most AMC complaints in Gurgaon trace to a missing handover, not lazy cleaners: nobody told the cleaner who owns the pantry, nobody said who buys the bin liners, nobody noticed absences until the office did. The handover costs us a morning and saves both sides a quarter of friction.
