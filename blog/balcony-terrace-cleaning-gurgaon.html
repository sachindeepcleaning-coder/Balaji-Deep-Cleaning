Pigeon-Proofing (What Actually Works)

  • Daily 2-minute dropping pickup — dried droppings etch tiles and breed fungus
  • Disinfectant wash monthly on railings and parapet ledges
  • Spikes/nets installed by vendors (we clean around them; we do not install)
  • Never sweep droppings dry — damp-wipe with mask on; airborne spores are the real hazard

The Monsoon Prep Pass Most Bookings Skip

  • Drain first — balcony drains are flushed and deodorised before tile work so scrub water has somewhere to go.
  • Planter zone — soil and dead leaves moved, pots lifted and the floor under them scrubbed; this is where rain stains start.
  • Railing and grill detail — rust spots treated, cobwebs pulled from grill corners before the first rain glues them on.
  • Glass and door tracks — tracks vacuumed and wiped; clogged tracks are the number-one monsoon water-entry complaint in Gurgaon flats.

A balcony cleaned in April behaves differently through July than one cleaned in June: drains are clear before the first downpour, tracks do not overflow, and the pigeon-dropping problem never gets its monsoon head start. Book the pass a few weeks before the rains; it is the same ₹300–₹800 as a standard clean, just better timed.

Pigeon Problems: Removal, Cleaning and Keeping Them Gone

  • Dropping removal — dried droppings are wetted and scraped, never swept dry (dried dust is the respiratory hazard); the zone is disinfected after.
  • Nest sites — AC outdoor units, ledge ends and grille corners are the three Gurgaon favourites; clearing them is standard, dispersing nesting birds is not attempted in breeding season.
  • Deterrence that works — spike strips on ledges and netting on grilles; ultrasonic gadgets are largely a refund waiting to happen.
  • Terrace drains — on terrace jobs, all drains are flushed after cleaning so the first rain tests clean pipes, not last month's sludge.

Pigeon work is a hygiene job, not a cosmetic one — dried droppings carry fungi that are genuinely dangerous when inhaled, which is why the wet-scrape-disinfect sequence is non-negotiable. Deterrence is separate: cleaning removes the evidence, spikes and nets remove the invitation, and doing only the first means booking the same job again in six weeks.
