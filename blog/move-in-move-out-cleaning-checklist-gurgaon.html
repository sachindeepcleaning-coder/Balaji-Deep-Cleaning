Why Professional Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning Matters

  • Landlords often require a clean handover for deposit refund
  • New tenants want a hygienic start without previous occupants' dust and stains
  • Empty homes reveal every corner that normal cleaning misses
  • Hard-water stains, kitchen grease and bathroom scale become clearly visible

Complete Checklist — What We Clean

Kitchen

  • Chimney and exhaust (filters cleaned)
  • Hob, burners and surrounding tiles
  • Cabinet exteriors and accessible interiors
  • Countertops, sink and taps
  • Fridge and microwave exteriors
  • Floor scrubbing

Bathrooms (all)

  • Toilet deep sanitization
  • Basin, taps and mirror
  • Tile and grout descaling
  • Shower area and glass
  • Exhaust fan
  • Floor

Bedrooms & Living Areas

  • All floors (sweep + mop / scrub)
  • Walls and skirting (cobwebs, marks)
  • Fans, lights and switchboards
  • Windowsills and accessible window tracks
  • Wardrobe exteriors
  • Under-bed and corner cleaning

Other Areas

  • Balconies and utility areas
  • Passages and lobby (if applicable)
  • Main door and handles

Optional Add-ons

  • Sofa / mattress shampoo
  • Carpet shampoo
  • Inside fridge / inside cabinets (if requested)

Our Process for Empty Homes

  1. Assessment — We note the condition and confirm scope.
  2. Top-to-bottom clean — Starting from upper surfaces down to floors.
  3. Detailed wet work — Kitchen and bathrooms receive full deep treatment.
  4. Final walkthrough — You (or the landlord) inspect every area.
  5. Checklist sign-off — Available on request for landlord records.

Tenant vs Owner — Different Checklists

Move-Out (Tenant): Prove It

  • Photograph every room before the team starts (timestamped)
  • Get the ticked checklist sheet signed at walkthrough
  • Keep the payment receipt with the job scope attached

Move-In (Owner/Tenant): Reset It

  • Book before furniture arrives — empty-flat access doubles the depth
  • Prioritise kitchen + bathrooms: previous-use hygiene is unknown
  • Run ACs and taps 10 minutes post-clean to flush loosened residue

Same-Day Handover Protocol

  1. Morning slot — team arrives with machines; landlord time fixed for evening
  2. Top-down clean — ceilings to floors so nothing re-soils finished zones
  3. Wet rooms mid-day — kitchen/bathrooms get dwell time for degreasers/descalers
  4. 4 PM walkthrough — owner inspects, flags, we fix on the spot, keys exchange
