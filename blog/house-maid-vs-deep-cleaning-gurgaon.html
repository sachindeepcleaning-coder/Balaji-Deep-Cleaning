How to Hire a Maid Without Getting Burned

  1. Use manpower agencies, not cleaning companies, for placement — different business, different liability.
  2. Meet the worker first; confirm language, hours, off-days and phone access.
  3. Verify replacement terms — who comes when she is absent, and in how many hours.
  4. Police verification on file before the first solo day at home.
  5. Start monthly, never yearly advances — salary monthly, 30-day exit both sides.
