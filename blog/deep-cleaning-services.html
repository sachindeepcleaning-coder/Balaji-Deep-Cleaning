Crew size, not effort, keeps a deep clean inside one day. Chemicals that need dwell time — descaler in bathrooms, degreaser on the hob — go on in the first hour so they work while hands are elsewhere, and floors are polished last so nothing re-dusts a finished room. A team that starts by mopping floors is an hourly service wearing a deep-cleaning label.
