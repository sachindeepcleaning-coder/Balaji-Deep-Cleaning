The Five-Point Test to Run on Any Service

  • Fixed or hourly? — hourly quotes that 'depend on condition' run 40% over estimate; fixed BHK pricing transfers the risk to the vendor.
  • Who owns the checklist? — the company's written list beats 'we do everything' verbal promises every time.
  • Verification — police-verified staff with ID on display; ask what happens if the same cleaner is unavailable on the revisit.
  • Chemicals — named-brand chemistry (Taski, Diversey class) versus unbranded acid; your fittings can tell the difference.
  • Payment terms — pay-after-walkthrough is the strongest signal a vendor trusts its own quality.

Run all five on any quote you hold. Two or three failures predict a bad experience even when reviews look fine — reviews capture one day's outcome, but pricing structure, checklist ownership and verification predict every visit.
