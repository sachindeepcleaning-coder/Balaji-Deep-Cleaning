The method is chosen per zone, not per building. Most cubicle carpet needs only low-moisture encapsulation — dry by morning, no cancelled workday. Traffic lanes by lifts and pantries get full extraction on a Friday night so the drying window eats into the weekend, not Monday.

Scheduling Around a Working Floor

  • Standard window — after 7 pm or weekends; low-moisture zones reopen same night, extraction zones by morning.
  • Zone scheduling — corridors and lift lobbies (the extraction zones) go last on Friday night so they dry over the weekend.
  • Furniture — workstations stay in place; chairs are lifted onto desks for the carpet pass and reset after.
  • Noise — extraction machines are loud; cubicle work with quiet encapsulation pads runs during low-occupancy hours if a day slot is unavoidable.

Office carpet cleaning fails on logistics more than on chemistry — a perfect extraction job that strands a team from their desks Monday morning is a failed job. The zone plan above is shared before booking, so your facilities team knows exactly which corners are wet, when, and for how long.
