Office Upholstery Is Not Home Upholstery

  • Fabric grade — office sofas are poly blends that take hotter extraction than delicate home linens; colour-fastness still tested on a hidden patch first.
  • Use pattern — reception sofas take 40+ contacts a day, so a deodorising pass after extraction is standard, not an upsell.
  • Access — cleaned in place; dragging pieces through corridors risks frame damage.
  • Timing — after 7 pm or weekends; 4–6 hour drying means a Friday-night slot is ready before Monday standup.

Office upholstery also carries food and coffee stains home sofas rarely see, and those set harder. Stain zones are pre-sprayed 15 minutes before extraction to lift what a single pass leaves — which is why the crew walks the sofas and marks stains before the machine comes out.
