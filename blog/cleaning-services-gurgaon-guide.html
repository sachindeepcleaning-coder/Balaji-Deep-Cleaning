Methods Professionals Use (and When Each Is Right)

  • Hot-water extraction — sofas, carpets, mattresses; flushes mites, stains and odour (our standard)
  • Steam sanitization — bathrooms, kitchens; kills germs without harsh residue
  • Acid descaling — taps, tiles, shower glass vs Gurgaon’s hard water (never on marble)
  • Dry / low-moisture clean — silk, velvet, delicate rugs that must not get wet
  • Machine scrubbing — vitrified, Kota and tile floors; NOT diamond marble polish (separate service)

Keep It Clean Between Visits (The 20/10 Rule)

  1. 20 minutes on, 10 off — daily micro-sessions beat monthly marathons
  2. Kitchen after cooking — hob wipe, chimney mesh check; stops grease baking on
  3. Bathroom 2-minute rule — squeegee glass, dry tap necks; halves limescale
  4. Entry discipline — mats in/out, shoe point; cuts tracked dust 70% near Dwarka Expressway corridors
  5. Split by day — dusting Monday, floors Wednesday, baths Friday; assign per person

What Is Never Included (Read Before You Pay Anyone)

  • Utensil washing and laundry (add-ons, not standard)
  • Wall repainting, putty, seepage or waterproofing repair
  • Marble diamond polishing (₹8–₹25/sq ft, separate machine job)
  • Inside sealed motors/appliances (chimney motor, AC coils)
  • High-rise rope-access facade (licensed crews only)
  • Pest-control spraying (different licence; book separately)
