Pantries & Café Counters at Premium Standard

Premium pantries are judged like show kitchens: quartz or stone counters needing matched degreasers, glass-front cabinets showing every smear, and coffee stations where Appointment-view mornings begin. Scope covers counter degrease by surface type, cabinet-glass polish, appliance exteriors, sink descaling and bin sanitization — with kettle and espresso-machine scale descaled, not wiped over. Under-cabinet lighting strips get dusted; they photograph badly when skipped.

Artwork, Glass Partitions & Statement Pieces

Golf Course Road receptions carry art frames, glass partitions and the occasional chandelier-adjacent lobby feature. Frames get dry detailing (never wet chemicals near canvas or prints), partitions get both-side streak-free cleaning with frame wipe, and high glass is reached with extension poles from the floor — no ladders on marble without mats. Anything requiring specialist restoration (scratched glass, tarnished brass) is logged as an exception, not improvised.
