Cyber City & DLF Phase 2 (Sector 24, NH-48)

Gurgaon's main IT corridor runs on tower discipline: gate passes issued against advance team IDs, loading-dock time slots, and night windows that typically open after 9 PM. Workstation density is the highest in the city — 60–120 seats per floor is normal — so the scope leads with desk-level detailing, keyboard-tray dust, cable-zone vacuuming and pantry turnaround, with glass partitions second. Book the access paperwork a day early; the cleaning itself is the easy part here.

Golf Course Road & Sector 43 (DLF Phase 5)

The Two Horizon–One Horizon belt trades dock chaos for finish sensitivity: stone lobbies, veneer partitions and designer pantry counters that punish wrong chemistry. Crews run marble-safe, pH-neutral products on stone and concierge-coordinated scheduling — tower lobbies announce vendor movement, so arrival times are kept to the minute. Washroom scope is identical to Sector 44; floor and lobby care is where the premium shows.

Institutional Area, Sector 32

Managed workspaces and institutional offices here run six-day weeks with daytime occupancy near 100%. That rules out weeknight section-work — the honest answer is a weekend-night full-floor pass. Meeting-room glass, pantry descaling and washroom banks get front-loaded in the shift plan so Monday 9 AM opens fresh. If your floor shares washrooms with other tenants, confirm the common-area boundary on the site visit to avoid scope disputes.

Udyog Vihar Phases I–V (Sector 20)

The industrial-commercial belt across Sector 20 brings a different soil profile: fine industrial dust on every horizontal surface and carpet tiles that hold a year of it. Here extraction leads — hot-water carpet shampoo and tile detailing before workstations, not after. The full Udyog playbook (phase-wise access, night logistics, AMC vs one-time math) lives in the Udyog Vihar office guide and the commercial AMC companion.

After-Hours Scheduling Without Losing a Workday

  • Access & alarm handover documented — keys, codes and alarm steps recorded before the shift, returned at walkthrough.
  • Supervisor on call all night — the floor is never left to an unsupervised crew, even on small offices.
  • Photo check-ins mid-shift — washroom and pantry photos shared on WhatsApp so morning holds no surprises.
  • Morning walkthrough before sign-off — your facility contact ticks the 9-area sheet; payment releases after, not before.
